## 4. AI Bubble Bust: the tech vs the business · 16 min

> **Cue (on screen):** Vote first. Then: which tool you use every day disappears first if it pops?

**Cards**

- **Post** (the board’s card): *AI Bubble Bust: AI Technology vs. AI as a Business*
- **Side** · The tech stays: Useful and still improving. A stock crash doesn't switch it off: the internet outlived dot-com.
- **Side** · The business pops: Huge spend, no clear way to earn it back. Vendors die, prices jump, tools vanish.

**Presenter notes** (their phone and Rehearse only)

*Opener:* Vote before you overthink it: does AI pop by 2028? Then the real question: which tool you use every day disappears first if it does?

- The post's split: AI stocks crashing is not AI going away. The tech is useful and still improving; the risk is companies spending huge sums with no clear way to earn it back.
- Dot-com is the template the post points at: plenty of companies died, the internet didn't. Ask the "pops" camp which AI vendor they'd bet on surviving, and why.
- Land it on our work: if your main AI tool doubled its price or shut down next quarter, what's the plan? One concrete answer beats ten opinions.

*Detail (Slack and the Run tab):* Posted anonymously, so the host frames it (3 min). Poll first, then the debate: a crash in AI stocks is not AI going away. Which side are you on, and what does it change about the tools we pick?
