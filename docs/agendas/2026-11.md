# November 2026 guild meeting: slides and Kers' script

Fri 13 Nov 2026, 11:00 IST · Desk Tour Month · **slides, not timed**: the
presenter moves on when a slide is done. The reference month for
[07-agenda-and-script-guide.md](../07-agenda-and-script-guide.md); the data is
in [2026-11.json](./2026-11.json).

**How it reads.** 25 slides in 7 sections. Each slide has a **cue** (one
line on screen), its **content** (posts and news as cards; sides and notes as
plain text) and the presenter's **notes** (opener and points: only on the
presenter's own phone and in Rehearse, never on the shared screen). Activity
slides open their poll or cloud on arrival and close it on leaving; the
results slide after each shows it big.

**Grounded in** the board as of 12 Nov: 5 posts (2 by members, both
anonymous and admin-selected; 3 by GuildBoard), 0 comments, 2 votes; 1 member
Radar link; 3 digest bytes with a vote.

**Show & Tell: dropped as a section.** Spotlight and Demo don't run in the
guild yet and nobody is confirmed, so a slot would be an empty promise on
screen. What earns its place instead: the board's voice-driven-development
post becomes "Steal a trick" right after the warm-up cloud (the cloud is
about tools, so it flows), with an optional volunteer two-minute screen share
in the notes; and the Wrap asks for one desk-tour volunteer for December.

**Memes:** four Kers memes, one opening each of Radar, the Topic, the clinic
and the Boss Round. Original, no images.
